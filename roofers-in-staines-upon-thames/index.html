In short

Staines has Victorian and Edwardian property near the town centre and river, with inter-war and post-war housing around it. Slate, tile, chimney work and a lot of riverside flat-roof extensions.

Roofing in Staines-upon-Thames

Staines runs from older Victorian and Edwardian property near the river and the High Street out to inter-war and post-war estates towards Stanwell. The riverside houses take more damp and more tree cover than most, so moss growth on north-facing slopes and gutters filling every autumn are routine rather than occasional. Older stacks here weather quickly for the same reason. Out towards Stanwell and the Ashford border the stock is largely tiled semis of the usual ages, with ridge bedding and slipped tiles the common calls. Being just south of the M25 with the crossing at Runnymede, Staines is about twenty five minutes from us, which makes it one of the Surrey towns we cover properly rather than occasionally.
