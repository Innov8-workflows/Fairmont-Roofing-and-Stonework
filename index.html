What we do

Roofing services in London and Surrey

From a single slipped slate to a full re-roof, and the chimney, lead and stone work that goes with it.

Roof Repairs

Slipped tiles, leaks, storm damage and failed flashing put right, usually in a single visit.

New Roofs and Re-roofing

Stripped back to the deck and rebuilt with new membrane, treated battens and fresh tiles or slate.

Slate and Heritage Roofing

Natural slate laid and dressed by hand, with lead ridges, hips and valleys formed on the roof.

Flat Roofing

EPDM rubber, fibreglass and felt systems for extensions, garages and dormers.

Chimneys and Leadwork

Repointing, rebuilds, cowls and new lead flashing where the old work has failed.

Stonework and Repointing

Stone stacks, copes and skews rebuilt, and weathered mortar joints raked out and pointed to match.

Project 01

Dormer apex stripped and re-slated with new leadwork

The old capping had rusted through at every joint and the slates around it had blown and cracked. The apex was stripped back, re-slated, and new lead ridges and hips were dressed and fixed on the roof.

Project 02

Full slate re-roof on a stone terrace

Decades of patching had left this roof a mixture of odd slates, moss and open joints. It came off in full, the roof was re-covered in new slate and the ridge line reinstated across the whole run.

Project 03

Garage flat roof re-covered in EPDM rubber

The felt had gone brittle, moss had taken hold and the trims were lifting at the edges. It was stripped to the deck and re-covered in a single sheet of EPDM with new trims all the way round.

Stripped to deckEPDM rubberNew edge trimsSingle sheet
Project 04

Extension flat roof stripped back and re-covered

A tired extension roof that had been patched more than once. This one was filmed from the strip out through to the finish: the old covering off, new boards down, then the EPDM rolled out in a single sheet and dressed up into an upstand against the brickwork.

Meet the owner

Fairmont Roofing and Stonework

A London and Surrey based roofing and stonework business covering the town and the surrounding areas. Pitched roofs, flat roofs, chimneys, leadwork and stone repairs, handled by the same people from the quote to the last sweep of the drive.

Tradition, craftsmanship and trust are on the badge for a reason. Slate is laid and lead is dressed on the roof by hand, and the parts of a job nobody will ever see are the parts that decide how long it lasts.

My passion for roofing started from watching my grandad and learning from the generations before me. Roofing was more than just a trade in my family, it was a skill, a way of life, and something to be proud of.

Sean McPherson Owner, Fairmont Roofing and Stonework
