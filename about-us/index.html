About Fairmont Roofing and Stonework

Fairmont Roofing and Stonework is run by Sean McPherson, working across London and Surrey on roofing, leadwork and stone.

In short

Fairmont Roofing and Stonework is a 2-person roofing and stonework business run by Sean McPherson, working across London and Surrey. Roofing came down through Sean's family, and the business is built around doing traditional work properly rather than quickly.

How it started

My passion for roofing started from watching my grandad and learning from the generations before me. Roofing was more than just a trade in my family, it was a skill, a way of life, and something to be proud of.

Growing up around that heritage inspired me to carry those traditions forward, build my own reputation, and create a roofing business based on quality, hard work and craftsmanship.

Meet Sean

I am the proud owner of Fairmont Roofing and Stonework, a business built on traditional values, quality workmanship and a genuine passion for the roofing and stonework trade.

My aim is simple: to provide every customer with honest advice, reliable service and workmanship I can be proud to put my name behind. I take great pride in attention to detail and treating every property as if it were my own.

Fairmont Roofing and Stonework is built on reputation, trust and quality, with every job completed to the highest possible standard.

What sets us apart

What sets Fairmont Roofing and Stonework apart is our commitment to quality, honesty and attention to detail. We do not believe in cutting corners or rushing jobs. We take pride in doing things properly, using quality materials and delivering workmanship that lasts.

We treat every customer and every property with the same level of care, whether it is a small repair or a complete roofing project. Our reputation is built on trust, reliability and the quality of the work we leave behind to inspire the next generation.
